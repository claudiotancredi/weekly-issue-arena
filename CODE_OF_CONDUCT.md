# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Weekly Issue Arena is a space where anyone can start contributing to open source, regardless of experience level. We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, issues, and pull requests
- Welcome newcomers — many participants are making their first open-source contribution
- Give credit where it's due
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Trolling or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Spamming issues, discussions, or PRs with unrelated content
- Gaming the point system through fake or trivial contributions
- Any behavior that would make others feel unwelcome

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all interactions within this repository, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other community spaces associated with the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported in the [Code of Conduct: Report a Concern](https://github.com/claudiotancredi/weekly-issue-arena/discussions/9) discussion. All reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly. The maintainer reserves the right to remove contributions, comments, or participants that violate this code of conduct.
